When it shows up

  • Right after the tour. The closing card offers it when the returned windows cross the threshold.
  • Crowded nudge. A tip under the menu bar icon, once per workspace per day, when a workspace holds more windows than sort-nudge and they no longer fit the layout.
  • Any time from the menu bar or a bind. Opening it never moves a window until you press Sort.

How it groups

  • By task maps bundle IDs to Code, Web, Chat, Media and Files, and picks a layout that fits the count.
  • By app gives the busiest apps their own workspace and folds the rest together.
  • Focus keeps the three windows you used last and moves everything else to the next free workspace.
  • Adding and removing. The + column (or +, or the next free number key) adds a workspace, up to 10. Removing one moves its windows to the workspace before it and renumbers the rest, with an undo right in the footer.
  • One display at a time. With more than one screen, the picker in the header chooses which display to sort, and focusmonitor keys switch it. A window sent to another display with movewindow, mon:±1 or dropped on its name lands in that screen's active workspace. It opens on the display under the pointer.
  • Windows that show the same Finder folder or document are offered for closing, never closed silently.

Config

Names, layouts and app groups you set here are remembered and reused next time.

# ~/.config/ouch/ouch.conf
sort-nudge = 8          # 0 turns the nudge off
sort-strategy = task    # task | app | focus
sort-group = Code, com.apple.dt.Xcode, com.mitchellh.ghostty
bind = $mod, o, sort

Prototype only. The desktop is drawn in the page; in Ouch the sorter is a floating panel over your real windows, and Sort runs the real tiler with its usual animation.
