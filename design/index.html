Primitives

Button

Button is the one push button: primary, secondary, ghost or danger, in three sizes.

  • One primary per surface; it carries the ↩ shortcut.
  • ghost for Skip, Back, Close and other ways out.
  • danger (on danger-soft) only for destructive actions, always with a verb ("Reset Shortcuts").
  • Icon-only buttons need label and a Tooltip.

Kbd Press H

Kbd renders keycaps for a shortcut, a chord, or a sequence of chords.

  • Small keycaps (size-kbd, 20px) for hints in tooltips, labels and buttons; menu rows use plain kbd text instead.
  • Large keycaps (40px, surface with a surface-sunken lip) only inside coach marks, where the real key press sinks them 2px onto tint.
  • Order modifiers the macOS way: ⌃ ⌥ ⇧ ⌘, then the key.

Symbol SF Symbols, driven by effect. Click to replay.

Symbol draws an SF Symbol by its real name and plays one of its effects.

  • Discrete effects (bounce, wiggle, rotate) report that something just happened; bind them to a counter.
  • Indefinite effects (pulse, breathe, variable) report a state that lasts; switch them off when it ends.
  • replace is for one control changing state (tiling on/off), never for unrelated icons.
  • Don't animate a symbol on every hover except scale in menu rows.

Controls

Row Open list: no box, hairlines between rows

Row is one line of a settings Group: optional symbol, label, description, and a control on the right.

  • Labels are sentence case and name the effect ("Focus follows mouse").
  • Put units and current values in the description, not the label.

Segmented

Segmented picks one of two to four options with a sliding thumb.

  • More than four options: use a Menu submenu or the layout thumbnail grid.

Slider Drag, hold Option for fine control, double-click to reset

Slider sets a number by dragging, in two shapes: line (thin track and thumb) and bar (a Control Center style pill whose fill is the indicator).

  • Tap to jump, drag to adjust. A press without moving sends the value to the press point and springs there, in both shapes. Dragging a line follows the pointer; dragging a bar moves relative to where you grabbed it, so grabbing never makes it jump.
  • Smooth under the hand. While dragging, the fill follows the pointer continuously and only the value rounds to step. A bar keeps its size while held: its track darkens a touch, its edge handle grows, and the value pops when it lands on a detent; the icon bounces at either end.
  • No ring on click. Pressing a slider focuses it without a focus ring, so arrow keys still work; the ring and value bubble show only when focus arrives from the keyboard.
  • Hold ⌥ while dragging to move at a tenth of the speed for fine values.
  • Detents. Within 2.5% of a tick the value snaps to it and the thumb gives a small spring bump. Moving to a value springs with a little overshoot, but landing on a tick (by tap or release) eases in without overshoot, so the handle settles exactly on the mark.
  • Rubber band. Dragging past either end stretches the control a few pixels (and thins it slightly) and springs back on release.
  • Double-click resets to the default. Arrow keys step (⇧ for ×10), Page Up/Down move a tenth, Home/End jump to the ends.
  • While dragging, the line's thumb grows, shows an accent core and an inverted value bubble; the bar's label and value switch from ink to on-accent exactly where the fill passes under them.
  • Use line inside settings rows and bar for a stack of related values (spring bounce, stiffness) where the label belongs inside the control.

Switch

Switch toggles one setting on or off immediately.

Click, Space or Return toggles it. The thumb can also be dragged: it follows the pointer, the track colour flips as it crosses the middle, and on release it springs to the nearer side.

  • Use for settings that apply instantly. For choices of 2–4 options use Segmented.

Surfaces

CommandMenu The menu, flattened by a query

CommandMenu is a Menu that opens with the search field first and is wider (360px).

  • Results group under their top-level parent and show the rest of the path as a breadcrumb.
  • Use it for ⌘K in Settings and the menu bar; the data is shared with the Menu so the two never disagree.

Menu Hover, arrows, or just type

Menu is the dropdown with groups, checks, radios, switches, inline controls and nested submenus, plus type-to-search.

  • Nest one level, two at most; put the current choice of a radio group in the parent row's value.
  • Every actionable row shows its shortcut. Give hidden aliases (hidden: true) to commands that live inside a custom control so search still finds them.
  • Hover opens after 100 ms with a 180 ms grace period; ↑↓ → ← ↩ esc work; typing focuses search.

Surface One floating pattern, five densities

Surface is the single floating container behind every menu, tooltip, tip, coach mark, HUD and welcome card.

  • Pick the density by job, not by size: tooltip xs, confirmation sm, menu or tip md, tour step lg, welcome xl.
  • Slots always appear in the same order; leave a slot out rather than reordering.
  • Anything with a button is md or larger. Coach marks always point at a real target with the caret.

Tooltip

Tooltip is the smallest Surface: a plain raised label, the same surface as menus, with an optional shortcut.

Wrap the target as children; provide label, optional keys/then, side (bottom, top, right) and delay (500 ms default). open forces it visible.

  • One short phrase in sentence case, no full stop. Never a button inside: use a tip (Surface md).
  • Every icon-only button gets one.
  • All tooltips share one height (28px), padding (5 × 8) and type. A shortcut is one chord at most; a sequence belongs in a tip.
  • A tooltip always points at its target with a small caret, centred on the side it opens from.
  • It uses the same pointer as every other surface: the rounded diamond, no extra border. Depth comes from the shared popover shadow.

Guidance

CoachMark A tour step. Press L.

CoachMark is one step of the keyboard tour: a plain raised Surface lg with step dots, a title, body, live keycaps and Next.

  • The step completes only when the real key is pressed; Next appears (with ↩) after.
  • Point the caret at the real menu bar item or practice window. Never dim or cover the desktop.
  • Kicker counts ("STEP 3 OF 6"); the done line is short ("Focus moved").

HUD Click to replay

HUD confirms a keyboard action in the middle of the screen: glass Surface with a layout thumbnail and its name.

  • Shown for about a second, never interactive, ignores the mouse.
  • The warning variant wiggles exclamationmark.triangle.fill and names the app that refused.

Tiling

LayoutPicker Hover a layout

LayoutPicker chooses one of the 13 layouts from a grid of thumbnails, with the hovered layout's name and description on one line below.

  • Thumbnails carry no labels, so nothing wraps; the info line truncates.
  • Used inside the Layout submenu and in Settings.
Tall Main on the left, the rest stacked on the right

LayoutThumb

LayoutThumb draws a miniature of one of the 13 tiling layouts.

  • Use the layout descriptions from LayoutThumb.layouts[k].desc as tooltips; they are the app's own copy.

WorkspaceStrip

WorkspaceStrip shows workspaces 1–9 (or 10) as pills; click to switch.

  • Current is filled accent with on-accent text. Workspaces with windows are ink at 500; empty ones are ink-faint at 400. Both read at 4.5:1, so no number fades out.

--ink-faint Placeholders and tertiary metadata. Meets 4.5:1 on surface, surface-raised and surface-sunken; disabled items use it at 60% opacity.
--accent The palette's brand fill: primary button, switch on, selected segment, progress, current workspace pill.
--accent-hover accent while hovered or pressed.
--on-accent Text and symbols on accent and accent-hover.
--accent-ink Accent used as text or a meaningful symbol: links, the Symbol showcase. On surface, surface-raised, accent-soft and wash-1. Never for hover or keyboard highlight.
--accent-soft Info callouts only. Holds ink and accent-ink. Never a hover or keyboard highlight: those use hover.
--tint The palette's quiet tone: illustration fills, layout-thumbnail panes, empty states.
--focus Keyboard focus ring, 2px solid with 2px offset. 3:1 on every surface.
--success Done states: tour step passed, permission granted. Always paired with checkmark.circle.fill.
--warning Window refuses to resize, config warnings. Always paired with exclamationmark.triangle.fill.
--danger Destructive menu items (Quit, Reset), errors. Always a word plus a symbol, never colour alone.
--danger-soft Ground for a highlighted destructive row and error callouts. Holds danger and ink.
--glass Translucent fill for floating chrome when real Liquid Glass is unavailable (web, pre-macOS 26).
--wash-1 Zen-style chrome wash, first stop (top-left): accent mixed into bg. Window chrome and sidebars sit on the wash; ink, ink-muted and accent-ink read on it.
--wash-2 Chrome wash, second stop (bottom-right): tint mixed into bg. Use as linear-gradient(140deg, wash-1, bg 55%, wash-2).
